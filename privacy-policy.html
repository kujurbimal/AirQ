AirQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Privacy at a glance: AirQ is designed to provide air-quality and weather information based on your selected or device location. AirQ does not require you to create an account.

1. Introduction

Welcome to AirQ ("AirQ", "we", "us", or "our"). AirQ is an air-quality and environmental information application that provides information such as air-quality conditions, AQI, particulate matter, weather conditions, maps, forecasts, and related environmental insights.

This Privacy Policy explains how AirQ handles information when you use our website or Android application.

By using AirQ, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Information

AirQ may request access to your device's location when you use features such as "My Location".

Location information may include approximate or precise geographic coordinates provided by your device, depending on the permission granted by you.

We use location information to provide location-specific services, including:

  • Air-quality information
  • AQI information
  • Weather information
  • Environmental forecasts
  • Map and location-based features

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. If location permission is denied, you may still be able to use AirQ by manually selecting a location, where supported.

2.2 Information You Provide

AirQ does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect information such as your name, postal address, telephone number, or password through the application.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

AirQ itself does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or user profiling.

However, third-party services used by the application may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, request information, or diagnostic information as part of providing their services.

3. How We Use Information

Information available to AirQ is used primarily to provide and improve the application's functionality.

This may include:

  • Displaying air-quality information for a location
  • Displaying weather conditions and forecasts
  • Displaying maps and environmental information
  • Providing AQI-related explanations and insights
  • Improving application reliability and performance
  • Responding to support requests

4. Third-Party Services

AirQ relies on third-party services to provide some of its functionality. These services may process information necessary to fulfill requests made by the application.

4.1 Open-Meteo

AirQ may use Open-Meteo services to obtain weather and air-quality information.

Requests may contain geographic coordinates or location-related parameters necessary to retrieve environmental data.

For more information, please review Open-Meteo's terms .

4.2 OpenStreetMap

AirQ may use OpenStreetMap-based services to display maps and geographic information.

Map requests may be processed by third-party infrastructure used to provide map tiles or geographic services.

More information about OpenStreetMap's privacy practices is available at: OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy .

4.3 Google Gemini / Google AI Services

AirQ may provide AI-powered explanations or environmental insights using Google Gemini or related Google AI services.

Information necessary to generate an AI response may be transmitted to the applicable AI service.

AirQ does not intentionally submit passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information to the AI service.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into AI-powered features.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Location Data and Retention

AirQ uses location information primarily to retrieve location-specific environmental and weather information.

AirQ does not intentionally maintain a personal location history associated with a user account.

Location information may nevertheless be processed temporarily by third-party services when AirQ requests weather, air-quality, map, or related information.

6. Data Sharing

AirQ does not sell personal information.

AirQ may transmit information to third-party service providers when necessary to provide application functionality, such as weather, air-quality, mapping, or AI services.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or governmental request.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by AirQ. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Users should understand that internet-based services may involve inherent security risks.

8. Children's Privacy

AirQ is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles or accounts.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Your Choices

You can control several aspects of AirQ's access to your device.

  • You can deny or revoke location permissions through Android settings.
  • You can stop using the application at any time.
  • You can manually select a location where that feature is available.

10. Health and Air-Quality Disclaimer

AirQ provides environmental and general wellness information. The information provided by AirQ is not intended to constitute medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare advice.

Air-quality measurements and forecasts may be based on modeled, estimated, or third-party environmental data and may not represent exact conditions at your specific location.

Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions, particularly when experiencing health symptoms or when dealing with conditions affected by air pollution.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in AirQ, applicable laws, or third-party services.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date displayed at the top of this page.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or AirQ's privacy practices, please contact us.
